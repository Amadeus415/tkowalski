Driving directions

From 101 North or South.

The building is on your left on Gate 5 Rd, with parking in the rear.

  1. Take Exit 445A toward Marin City / Sausalito.
  2. After exiting, drive toward Sausalito.
  3. At the first signal, turn right onto Bridgeway.
  4. Continue on Bridgeway to the next signal, Gate 5 Rd.
  5. Turn left onto Gate 5 Rd.
  6. 4000 Bridgeway is on your left. Park in the rear.
  7. Suite 301 is on the ground floor, behind the hemispherical windows.
