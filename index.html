About Tom

Four decades of measuring what people can do.

Tom A. Kowalski, OTR/L, has spent his career establishing objective analyses of functional limitations and competency, from microsurgical hand rehabilitation to medical-legal evaluation.

  • Former Hand Therapy / Rehabilitation Coordinator, UCLA Medical Center, Division of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery / Microsurgical Replantation Service
  • Published in the Journal of Hand Surgery, Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery and the Journal of Reconstructive Microsurgery
  • Prior invited reviewer for the Journal of the American Congress of Rehabilitation Medicine
  • Certified Ergonomics Assessment Specialist (CEAS), with additional certification in hand therapy and physical agent modalities

Make a referral

Three steps from referral to report.

  1. Send the referral

    Email or fax the referral with the current position description. If there isn't one, a job analysis can be completed before the FCE.

  2. We schedule the evaluation

    At the Sausalito office, or at the worksite when the position calls for an on-site FCE.

  3. You receive the report

    Clear, objective findings within 72 business hours. Brief return-to-work evaluations can include a same-day report.
